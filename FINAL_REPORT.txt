root cause: The prior scenario (scenario 4 - REVIEW mode) did not consume its final test provider step. Thus, when scenario 5 (REAL CANCELLATION) pushed its provider step and began its turn, it incorrectly consumed the leftover `"done review"` step. Because that step completed instantly, the async cancellation function was never executed. The test arbitrarily waited 500ms and then issued the cancel, but the turn had already finished. Clearing `provider.steps` between tests and using a deterministic `providerStarted` readiness handshake guarantees the test properly waits for the provider to become active and registers its abort listener.
